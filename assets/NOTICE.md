# Selected Financial Services asset attribution and boundaries

The bundled Agent prompts and Skills are limited to the three selected main Agents, their nine owned Workers, and the 22 Skills listed in `runtime-scope.json`. Their selected origins are recorded in `runtime-manifest.json`. The full historical multi-domain source archive and excluded-domain asset copies have been removed.

The original financial-services source is licensed under Apache License 2.0. `runtime-skills/skill-creator/LICENSE.txt` is retained with its Skill asset. No commercial data, credentials, publication, client communication, formal ledger posting, trading, funds movement, or compliance sign-off is authorized by Skill access. Runtime permissions and configured provider availability remain authoritative; missing templates are not invented or claimed to be included.
